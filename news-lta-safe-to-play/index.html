Padel Power Achieves LTA “Safe to Play” Registered Venue Status

Club News·18 March 2026·Padel Power

We’re proud to announce that Padel Power Northants Ltd will officially open on 3rd April, launching as an LTA Safe to Play Registered Venue for 2025/26.

Achieving this accreditation before opening is a major milestone for our team and highlights our commitment to delivering a safe, inclusive, and professionally run environment for everyone who steps onto court.

What It Means to Be “Safe to Play”

The Lawn Tennis Association’s “Safe to Play” standard is designed to ensure venues across the UK provide the highest levels of safeguarding, coaching, and inclusivity.

As part of this recognition, Padel Power has committed to:

  • A comprehensive safeguarding policy in place for all players and visitors
  • A clear equity, diversity and inclusion policy
  • A dedicated trained Welfare Officer overseeing safeguarding
  • LTA-accredited coaches (Level 2–5) delivering all coaching sessions

These standards ensure that every player, regardless of age, ability or experience, can enjoy padel in a safe and welcoming environment.

Setting the Standard from Day One

Opening as an LTA Registered Venue means we are setting the tone from the very beginning.

Our goal is not just to bring padel to Northampton, but to do it properly, creating a venue that players can trust, where safety and quality are at the heart of everything we do.

A New Chapter for Padel in Northampton

Located at Unit 1, Kingsfield Business Park, Padel Power is ready to become a leading destination for the sport in the region.

From day one, players can expect:

  • A professionally run venue aligned with national standards
  • A welcoming and inclusive environment
  • A focus on player wellbeing and experience

See You on Court – 3rd April

With opening day just around the corner, we can’t wait to welcome the Northampton community onto court for the very first time.

Padel Power is ready. Game on. 🎾
