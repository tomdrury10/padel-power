Getting started

The
basics

What is Padel Power?+
A premium indoor padel club in Northampton with state-of-the-art courts, coaching, leagues, social play and a genuinely welcoming community.
Where are you located?+
Unit 1, Kingsfield Business Park, Gladstone Road, Northampton, NN5 7PP. Free parking on site, five minutes from the town centre.
Do I need experience to play padel?+
None at all. Complete beginners, casual players and competitive athletes are all welcome, and beginner sessions run every week.
Are the courts indoors?+
Yes, every padel court is fully indoor, so you can play year-round whatever the weather is doing outside.
How many courts do you have?+
Ten in total: seven double courts, two single courts and one pickleball court.
Can I rent a racket?+
Yes, racket rental is £3 per session, and it's free on selected membership and loyalty tiers.
Do you have changing rooms and social areas?+
Yes. Modern changing facilities with hot showers and Dyson hairdryers, spectator viewing areas, a lounge and an on-site bar.
Booking & membership

Courts &
costs

How do I book a court?+
Bookings are live on Playtomic, where you can check real-time availability and book your court in seconds.
Do I need a membership to play?+
No. Anyone can book. Members get an 18-day priority booking window, discounted rates and access to exclusive leagues for £145 a year.
What are your opening hours?+
6am to midnight Monday to Friday, and 6am to 11pm on weekends.
Can I play casually or socially?+
Absolutely. Courts are available for casual play any time, and we run organised social padel sessions if you want to meet other players.
Do you have a loyalty scheme?+
Yes. The Padel Power Loyalty App tracks every minute you play and converts it to points for free court time, bar and pro shop discounts, coaching discounts, equipment and member events.
Coaching & competition

Getting
better

Do you offer coaching?+
Yes, professional coaching for all levels, from your first ever session to advanced competition prep, through the Padel Power Academy.
What coaching options are there?+
Individual and group sessions, beginner to advanced programmes, junior and student pathways, plus corporate packages.
What is the Padel Power Academy?+
A structured coaching pathway designed to build your skills, confidence and matchplay ability over time, rather than one-off lessons.
Do you run leagues?+
Yes, all year round: men's, women's, mixed doubles, veterans, students, juniors and corporate leagues. Browse and join directly on Playtomic.
How do I stay updated?+
Join the WhatsApp community, follow @padelpower.uk on Instagram and TikTok, or check the News page.
