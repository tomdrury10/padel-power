Padel leagues launching from April

Club News·4 February 2026·Padel Power

We’re excited to announce that padel leagues at Padel Power will launch from April, bringing competitive and social indoor padel to players across Northamptonshire.

Leagues will form the heartbeat of the club, creating regular match play, friendly rivalries and a strong padel community for players of all abilities.

Whether you’re completely new to padel or an experienced competitor, there will be a league designed for you.

What Leagues Will Be Available?

From launch, Padel Power will run a full calendar of indoor padel leagues, including:

  • Men’s Leagues, competitive play across multiple levels
  • Women’s Leagues, supportive, social and competitive formats
  • Mixed Doubles Leagues, perfect for pairing up and meeting new players
  • Veterans Leagues, smart padel and a great community atmosphere
  • Student Leagues, affordable and competitive padel for university and college players
  • Juniors Leagues, helping the next generation develop their game
  • Corporate & Social Leagues, ideal for friends, families and workmates

All leagues will be played on premium indoor padel courts, ensuring consistent playing conditions year-round.

Who Are the Leagues For?

Padel Power leagues are designed to be:

  • Welcoming to new players
  • Competitive for experienced players
  • Well organised and clearly structured
  • Social, friendly and enjoyable

Leagues aren’t just about winning, they’re about regular play, improvement, and being part of a growing padel community in Northampton.

When Do Leagues Start?

Padel leagues at Padel Power will begin from April.

Full details on league formats, divisions, schedules and pricing will be released closer to launch.

How to Get Involved

League sign-ups will open ahead of April.
