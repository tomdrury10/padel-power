How it works

Two ways
to play

Pick whichever suits the day. Both are booked in advance so there's never a queue at the gate.

01
Supervised session

Drop them off with a member of our team and go and play. Sessions run with a minimum of three children and a maximum of ten, so every child gets watched properly. Session times and prices are announced at launch.

02
Hire it yourselves

Book the space for your own crew at £5 per child per hour. A parent or carer stays in the soft play and supervises. Ideal for a couple of families, a playdate or a birthday morning.

Common questions

Before you
book

When does it open?+
In the next few weeks. The date and the first session times will be posted here and on our Instagram and Facebook. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you directly.
Do I need to book?+
Yes, for both supervised sessions and self-supervised hire. Supervised sessions need at least three children booked to run and take a maximum of ten.
Can I leave my child and go and play?+
In a supervised session, yes. A member of our team looks after the group while you're on court or in the gym. If you hire the space yourselves, a parent or carer stays in the soft play and is responsible for the children.
What ages is it for?+
Roughly three to eight. If your child is a little outside that and you're not sure, ask us and we'll tell you honestly whether they'll enjoy it.
Is there food and drink?+
The café is a few steps away with fresh food, proper coffee and snacks for grown-ups and children alike.
