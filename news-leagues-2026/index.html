Padel Power leagues coming in 2026

Club News·23 November 2025·Padel Power

As we prepare to open our brand-new indoor padel centre in early 2026, we’re excited to share the full lineup of leagues that will be available at Padel Power. Our aim is to create a vibrant and inclusive padel community, offering structured competition and social play for every type of player, from beginners to experienced competitors.

Here’s a first look at the leagues we’ll be launching:

Men’s League

Fast-paced, competitive and open to all levels. Whether you’re new to padel or an experienced player, this league delivers great matches, progression and plenty of action.

Women’s League

Supportive, social and competitive. A dedicated space for women to develop their game, meet other players and enjoy high-quality, structured match play.

Mixed Doubles League

Grab a partner and take on new challengers each week. Ideal for couples, friends or anyone who loves the social side of padel.

Veterans League

Designed for experienced players who value smart play, tactical matches and strong community spirit. A great way to stay active and competitive.

Student League

Affordable, social and competitive padel for university and college players. A brilliant mix of friendly rivalry and high-energy gameplay.

Juniors League

The perfect place for the next generation of padel stars. Focused on fun, development and match experience for younger players.

Corporate & Social Leagues

Ideal for workmates, families or groups of friends. Bring your team together, compete for bragging rights and enjoy padel in a relaxed, social environment.

Why Leagues Matter at Padel Power

Leagues are at the heart of what we’re building. They help players improve, create structure, form new friendships and build a true community feel within the club. With leagues for every age and ability, there’s a place for everyone at Padel Power.

Register Your Interest

League registrations will open ahead of our launch. Stay tuned for updates on how to secure your spot.

Padel Power is coming, and we can’t wait to get you on court.
