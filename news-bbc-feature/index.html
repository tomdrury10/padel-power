BBC Shines a Spotlight on Padel Power: Our Plans Make Headlines!

Press·25 November 2025·Padel Power

We’re excited to announce that Padel Power has been featured by BBC News, shining a major spotlight on our vision to bring a best-in-class padel centre to Northampton. This coverage marks another huge milestone in our journey and reflects the growing interest in padel, one of the fastest-rising sports in the UK.

Bringing Padel to Northampton

In their recent article, BBC Northamptonshire highlighted our proposal to transform a vacant warehouse in Northampton into a modern, purpose-built padel centre. Featuring panoramic courts, social spaces, coaching programmes, and community events, the facility is designed to open the sport up to everyone, from complete beginners to experienced players.

The BBC reported that our venue could open as early as the first quarter of next year, and we’re proud to confirm that Padel Power remains on track for a Q1 2026 launch.

Why This Feature Matters

Being recognised by the BBC is more than just good publicity. It’s a major moment for the padel community in Northamptonshire. Media attention helps raise awareness of:

  • The rapid growth of padel across the UK
  • The benefits of the sport for fitness and social connection
  • The excitement building around Padel Power’s upcoming indoor venue

This spotlight helps us push our mission forward: to create a social, energetic, and inclusive hub for padel lovers and newcomers alike.

Padel: A Sport That’s Taking Off

As the BBC describes, padel blends the best of tennis and squash: fast-paced, fun, and easy to learn. Its accessibility is one of the main reasons we chose to invest in bringing this sport to Northampton. Anyone can pick up a racket and start enjoying rallies within minutes.

What’s Next for Padel Power?

Over the coming months, we’ll be sharing updates on:

  • Construction and interior developments
  • Early membership opportunities
  • Community launch events
  • Coaching programmes and leagues

This is just the beginning, and the buzz created by the BBC feature only fuels our momentum.

Read the full story on BBC News: search “Plans to transform Northampton warehouse into padel court centre”.

Thank You for Your Support

We’re overwhelmed by the enthusiasm already surrounding Padel Power. Your support is helping us build something truly special for the Northamptonshire community.

Stay tuned: 2026 is set to be an unforgettable year for padel in Northampton!
