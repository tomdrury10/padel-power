How to Book a Padel Court at Padel Power

Guides·18 May 2026·Padel Power

Padel is growing rapidly across Northampton, which means one thing: courts can book up fast.

Whether you’re planning your first game with friends, joining a social session, or organising a weekly match, knowing how to book courts quickly and easily makes a huge difference.

The good news is that booking padel has become incredibly simple thanks to modern apps and online systems.

At Padel Power, all courts are booked through the popular Playtomic app, the world’s leading padel booking platform used by clubs and players across the UK and Europe.

In this guide, we’ll walk you through exactly how to book a court, how Playtomic works, and a few tips to help you secure the best time slots.

What Is Playtomic?

Playtomic is the main app used by many padel clubs to manage:

  • court bookings
  • player matching
  • social games
  • memberships
  • tournaments
  • coaching sessions

Think of it as the go-to app for padel players.

Instead of calling clubs or waiting for confirmations, everything happens instantly through your phone:

  • browse available courts
  • book sessions
  • invite friends
  • split payments
  • join public games
  • manage cancellations

For regular players, it quickly becomes an essential part of playing the sport.

Step 1: Download the Playtomic App

Getting started only takes a few minutes.

Simply:

  • Download Playtomic from the App Store or Google Play
  • Create a free account
  • Add your basic details
  • Set your location preferences

Once you’re signed in, you can immediately start browsing available courts and sessions nearby.

Step 2: Find Padel Power on Playtomic

Use the search bar inside the app to find: Padel Power Northampton

From there, you’ll be able to:

  • view available courts
  • check pricing
  • browse available time slots
  • book social sessions
  • join open matches
  • view coaching availability

The app updates availability in real time, making it easy to grab cancellations or newly released slots.

Step 3: Choose Your Court and Time

One of the best things about Playtomic is how easy it is to see availability.

You can filter by:

  • date
  • time
  • court type
  • match format
  • available players

Peak evening and weekend sessions tend to book fastest, especially during winter months, so booking a few days ahead is recommended.

If your schedule is flexible, weekday daytime sessions are often quieter and easier to secure.

Step 4: Invite Friends or Join a Game

Padel is usually played as doubles, so you’ll normally need four players.

Playtomic makes this incredibly simple.

You can:

  • invite friends directly through the app
  • split payment automatically
  • create private games
  • join public matches with other players

This is one of the reasons padel grows so quickly socially: players can easily organise games and meet others with similar skill levels.

For beginners, joining open social sessions is one of the easiest ways to get started.

Why Online Booking Has Changed Padel

The simplicity of apps like Playtomic has helped padel grow much faster than traditional racquet sports.

Everything feels quicker and more connected:

  • instant bookings
  • easy group organisation
  • last-minute games
  • player matching
  • social interaction

You’re not just booking a court, you’re joining a wider playing community.

For many players, checking Playtomic becomes part of their daily routine:

  • organising matches
  • seeing who’s playing
  • joining leagues
  • improving rankings
  • finding new opponents

It makes the sport feel active and accessible at all times.

The Social Side Starts Before You Even Arrive

One of the biggest strengths of padel is how social it feels, and that often begins during the booking process itself.

Through Playtomic, players regularly:

  • meet new playing partners
  • organise recurring weekly games
  • join beginner sessions
  • enter tournaments
  • connect with local players

At Padel Power, that community feel continues off the court too.

The club has been designed around the social side of the sport, with one of Northampton’s best padel social spaces. The café and bar sit directly beside the courts, allowing players to enjoy drinks, coffee, or post-match food while still watching the action unfold.

That atmosphere creates a much more connected experience than simply turning up, playing, and leaving immediately afterwards.

Players stay:

  • to watch matches
  • socialise with friends
  • organise future games
  • meet new people
  • enjoy the club environment

For many people, this becomes one of the best parts of playing regularly.

Tips for Booking the Best Time Slots

If you want the most popular court times, here are a few useful tips:

Book Early

Evening sessions and weekends can fill quickly.

Enable Notifications

Playtomic alerts you to cancellations and newly available courts.

Play Off-Peak When Possible

Weekday mornings and afternoons are often quieter and easier to book.

Join Social Games

Great for beginners and solo players looking to meet others.

Save Favourite Clubs

This makes rebooking much faster.

What You Need Before Arriving

Before your session, make sure you have:

  • comfortable sportswear
  • non-marking court shoes
  • water bottle
  • your booking confirmation

Don’t worry if you don’t own equipment yet, many clubs, including Padel Power, offer racket hire for beginners.

It’s designed to make getting started as easy as possible.

Final Thoughts: Booking Padel Has Never Been Easier

One of the reasons padel continues to grow so quickly is how simple the entire experience has become.

With apps like Playtomic, booking games takes minutes. Organising matches is easy. Joining social sessions is effortless. And finding new people to play with happens naturally.

At Padel Power, the combination of easy booking, high-quality courts, and a vibrant social atmosphere creates an experience that goes far beyond simply hiring a court.

Whether you’re trying padel for the first time or already playing weekly, your next game is only a few taps away.
