How to join

Two minutes
in the app

Memberships are sold through the Padel Power app, the same one that earns you loyalty points on court. Sign up, pay and get your entry QR code all in one place.

01
Download the app

Get the Padel Power app from the App Store or Google Play using the buttons below. Already have it? Skip to the next step.

02
Create your account

Sign up with your name, email and mobile. If you already have an account, just sign in.

03
Pick a gym membership

Choose Adult, Over 65, Student or Junior and pay in the app. It renews monthly and you can cancel whenever you like.

04
Scan and train

Your personal QR code appears in the app. Scan it at the gym entrance and you're in.

Good to know

Before you
join

Do I have to play padel to join the gym?+
No. Gym membership stands on its own and is separate from padel membership.
Is there a joining fee or a contract?+
Neither. Every membership is rolling monthly with no joining fee and no long-term commitment. Cancel in the app whenever you like.
How do I get in?+
With the QR code in the Padel Power app. Scan it at the gym entrance each visit. It works across all opening hours, from 5am on weekdays.
Who are the junior memberships for?+
Juniors aged 14 to 17. Message us on WhatsApp if you are not sure which membership fits.
Can I look around before joining?+
Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a time to show you round.
Is there parking and somewhere to eat?+
Free parking outside, and a bar and café inside where you can sit down after training. A soft play for the little ones is opening soon.
