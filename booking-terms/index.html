Booking Terms

Legal·Reformer Pilates classes

These terms cover reformer Pilates class bookings at Padel Power, Unit 1 Kingsfield Business Park, Gladstone Road, Northampton, NN5 7PP. Padel Power is a trading name of Padel Power Northants Limited, company number 16445182, registered at the same address. Booking a class means you accept them.

Booking and payment

Classes are booked online through our website. Each class is 1 hour and costs £20, paid by card at the time of booking. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Classes can be booked up to 14 days ahead. Online booking closes 24 hours before each class starts so instructors know who is coming. Each booking reserves one reformer bed for one person.

Cancelling your booking

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your class using the cancellation link in your booking confirmation text. Your payment is refunded in full to the card you paid with. Refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 working days.

Within 24 hours of the class, online cancellation is closed. If you cannot make it, call the club on 07595 250776. Payments for cancellations within 24 hours, and for missed classes, are not normally refunded.

If we cancel a class

A minimum of 3 bookings is required for a class to go ahead. If a class is not going ahead, you will receive a text notification 24 hours before the start and your payment will be refunded in full automatically.

If we have to cancel a class for any other reason, everyone booked on is notified and refunded in full.

In the studio

Please arrive 10 minutes before your class starts. Your instructor will set your reformer up. Grip socks are required and are available in the pro shop.

Classes are suitable for all levels. If you have an injury or a medical condition, tell your instructor before the class begins, and speak to your doctor first if you are unsure whether reformer Pilates is right for you.

Your details

We use your name, phone number and email to manage your booking and to send booking and cancellation messages. See our privacy policy for how your details are handled.

Contact

Questions about a booking or these terms: call 07595 250776 or use the contact form.
