How it works

Two ways
to play

Which one you get depends on the day. Both are booked in advance, in 60, 90 or 120 minute sessions, so there is never a queue at the gate.

Sat–Sun
Supervised · 08:00 to 16:00

Weekends are run by our team. Drop the children off and go and play, train or sit down with a coffee. Sessions need a minimum of three children and take up to ten, so everyone gets watched properly.

Mon–Fri
Unsupervised · 09:00 to 17:00

Midweek sessions are unsupervised, so a parent or carer stays in the room and looks after their own children. Supervised midweek sessions are coming soon.

Common questions

Before you
book

When does it open?+
In the next few weeks. The date and the first session times will be posted here and on our Instagram and Facebook. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you directly.
Do I need to book?+
Yes, for every session. Pick 60, 90 or 120 minutes, with two hours the longest booking. Supervised sessions need at least three children booked to run and take a maximum of ten.
Can I leave my child and go and play?+
At the weekend, yes. Saturday and Sunday sessions from 08:00 to 16:00 are supervised by our team while you are on court or in the gym. Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00, sessions are unsupervised, so a parent or carer stays in the room and is responsible for their own children. Supervised midweek sessions are coming soon, and these arrangements may change as we settle in.
What are the rules?+
Grip socks only, for children and for any grown-up going in with them. You can buy a pair at reception if you need one. No food or drink in the play zone itself. The rest of the house rules are on the wall by the gate.
Can we book it for a party?+
Parties and exclusive hire are arranged by email rather than online. Tell us the date and how many children are coming at info@padelpower.uk and we will come back to you.
What ages is it for?+
Roughly three to eight. If your child is a little outside that and you're not sure, ask us and we'll tell you honestly whether they'll enjoy it.
Is there food and drink?+
The café is a few steps away with food, proper coffee and snacks for grown-ups and children alike. It all stays outside the play zone though, nothing to eat or drink goes in there.
