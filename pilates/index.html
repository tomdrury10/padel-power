Good to know

First time on
a reformer?

I've never done Pilates. Can I just book any class?+
Yes. Every class is suitable for complete beginners. Your instructor tailors the session to who is in the room, sets your reformer up and guides you through.
What do I wear and bring?+
Comfortable gym wear and grip socks (available in the pro shop). Everything else, including the reformer, is provided.
What if my class doesn't reach 3 bookings?+
A minimum of 3 bookings are required for a class to go ahead. If yours is not going ahead you'll get a text notification 24 hours before the start.
Can I cancel a booking?+
Yes, up to 24 hours before class via the club. Within 24 hours, message us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to fill your bed.
