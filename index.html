Before you ask

Quick
answers

I've never played padel. Can I still come?+
Yes, and you'll be in good company. Beginner sessions run every week, staff will happily explain the rules, and nobody cares if your first lob hits the roof.
Do I need my own racket?+
No. Rental rackets are £3 a session, free on selected membership and loyalty tiers, and the pro shop has demo rackets when you're ready to buy.
Do I need a membership to book?+
No, anyone can book. Members get an 18-day priority booking window and discounts on every session for £145 a year.
How do I book?+
Everything runs through the Playtomic app: real-time availability, split payments with your group, and open matches if you need players.
