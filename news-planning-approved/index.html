Planning approved for indoor padel in Northampton

Club News·20 February 2026·Padel Power

Planning permission has officially been approved for Padel Power, Northampton’s new premium indoor padel centre.

This major milestone confirms that Northampton will soon be home to a state-of-the-art indoor padel facility, purpose-built to serve the rapidly growing padel community across Northamptonshire.

Following formal approval from West Northamptonshire Council, construction will now begin immediately, bringing high-quality indoor padel courts to Northampton for the first time at this scale.

A New Era for Indoor Padel in Northampton

Padel is one of the fastest-growing sports in the UK, and demand for indoor padel courts in Northampton has never been higher.

Padel Power will deliver a premium indoor padel club designed for players of all abilities, from beginners discovering padel for the first time to experienced competitors playing league and tournament padel.

Phase One of the Northampton development will include:

  • Nine indoor padel courts in Northampton
  • Pickleball and table tennis facilities
  • A café and bar for post-match socialising
  • High-spec changing facilities
  • Spectator and social viewing areas

This will establish Padel Power as Northamptonshire’s flagship indoor padel centre.

Construction Begins, Opening April 2026

With courts already ordered in advance of approval, construction will begin immediately to ensure a smooth build process.

The target opening date is 3rd April 2026, marking the launch of Northampton’s premier indoor padel club.

As interest in padel in Northampton continues to grow, the facility will provide year-round indoor play, removing weather disruption and offering a professional standard playing environment.

More Than Just Padel

Padel Power is being built as a long-term sports and social destination for Northampton.

Phase Two of the project will introduce:

  • A Reform Pilates studio
  • A fully equipped gymnasium

By combining indoor padel courts with fitness and social spaces, Padel Power will become a central hub for sport and community in Northamptonshire.

Join Northampton’s Growing Padel Community

Planning approval marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter for padel in Northampton.

Players, supporters and future members are encouraged to follow updates as construction progresses. Early access opportunities, Founders memberships and league information will be released in the coming months.
