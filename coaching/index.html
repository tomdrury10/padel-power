Padel Power Academy

Coached by people
who love this game

The Padel Power Academy is a structured coaching pathway built to grow your skills, confidence and matchplay ability. Led by Head Coach Max Graham, sessions run for every level: complete beginners, improvers chasing consistency, and advanced players prepping for competition.

Book a session → Ask a question
Programmes

Pick your
pathway

LEVEL / 01

Beginner

Never held a racket? Ideal. Grip, rules, serves and rallies in relaxed group sessions where everyone is new too.

LEVEL / 02

Improver

You can rally; now build consistency. Positioning, shot selection, and the lob that wins points at this level.

LEVEL / 03

Advanced

Tactical training and competitive matchplay. Sharpen patterns, pressure play and preparation for leagues and tournaments.

LEVEL / 04

Juniors & Students

Programmes for young players, plus school and college partnerships. Skills, confidence and a sport for life.

LEVEL / 05

One-to-one

Individual or shared private sessions built entirely around your game, at times that suit you.

LEVEL / 06

Corporate

Team-building your team will actually thank you for. Courts, coaching and the bar afterwards, all arranged.
