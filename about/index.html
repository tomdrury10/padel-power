The Club

A warehouse,
reborn

Padel is one of the fastest-growing sports in the world, but high-quality indoor courts are still hard to find. So we took an unused warehouse in Northampton and turned it into the club we wished existed: premium courts, proper coaching, and a social space you'd happily spend a whole evening in.

Every court is fully indoor with pro-level lighting and consistent playing conditions, all year round. Rain, wind and dark evenings are someone else's problem.
