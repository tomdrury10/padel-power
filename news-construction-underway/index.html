Construction underway at Padel Power

Club News·2 March 2026·Padel Power

Big news, Northampton… it’s officially happening!

Construction is now underway at Padel Power, the brand-new indoor padel venue opening in April 2026.

Located at:

Unit 1, Kingsfield Business Park

Gladstone Road

Northampton

NN5 7PP

We’re transforming this space into Northampton’s home of indoor padel, and we couldn’t be more excited.

Bringing Indoor Padel to Northampton

Padel is one of the fastest-growing sports in the UK, and soon you won’t need to travel far to play.

If you’ve been searching for:

  • Padel Northampton
  • Indoor padel Northampton
  • Padel courts Northampton
  • Padel near me

Your wait is nearly over.

Padel Power will offer:

  • Premium indoor panoramic courts
  • Professional-grade playing surfaces
  • High-performance lighting for year-round play
  • Coaching for beginners and advanced players
  • Social leagues & competitive tournaments
  • Junior development programmes
  • Corporate events & private hire

Whether you’re picking up a racket for the first time or you’re already hooked, this will be the place to play.

Construction Is Officially Underway

The build has begun at Kingsfield Business Park, and the transformation is in full swing. Over the coming weeks, our industrial unit will evolve into a modern, high-energy indoor padel venue designed for all-weather play.

Opening April 2026

From April 2026, players across Northampton and Northamptonshire will be able to experience the fastest-growing sport in the world, right here on their doorstep.

Padel Power isn’t just about courts. It’s about community, competition and creating a new sporting hub for the town.

We can’t wait to welcome you.
