Looking for reformer Pilates classes in Northampton? Padel Power is opening a brand-new eight-bed reformer studio inside the club this September, with expert instructors and classes for every level from 6:30am.

Why Padel Power

A studio built for
small classes

Eight beds, never more

Classes are capped at eight people, so your instructor sees every rep and corrects every position. One-hour sessions that work everything.

A class for every level

Every class is a one hour reformer session tailored by your instructor to who is in the room, from complete beginners to experienced participants.

Inside the club

The studio sits inside Padel Power, alongside the courts, bar and café. Free on-site parking and classes from 6:30am, so you can train before work.
